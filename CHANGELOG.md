## [2.0.0] — 2026-10-24

### Added
- Full rebuild as an assistive camera for Color Vision Deficiency (CVD).
- Live WebRTC viewfinder with front/rear camera switching.
- Daltonization correction filters for Protanopia, Deuteranopia, and Tritanopia, plus a high-contrast edge mode.
- CVD *simulation* modes so people with typical color vision can see what color-blind users see.
- Tap-to-inspect pixel eyedropper with nearest named color, HEX/RGB readout, and text-to-speech.
- Built-in Ishihara-style test plate, photo upload fallback, snapshot capture, and local history gallery.

### Fixed
- Color swatch now updates instantly, without transition lag.
